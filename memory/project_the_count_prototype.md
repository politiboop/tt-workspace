`/Users/brock/dev/politiboop/prototypes/the-count/` holds a standalone prototype page built 2026-10-26 from the corruption ledger ([[project-corruption-chronology]]): `build.cjs` parses the markdown ledger into `ledger.json` (rerun it after any ledger change), `index.html` renders it in the Deep Dives design language, `README.md` documents it. Serve it with the `the-count` entry in `.claude/launch.json` (port 4341). Nothing on the page is hand-typed data; every number is computed from the JSON and every link is one of the ledger's.

**Why:** The user asked on 2026-10-26 for a "fun and creative storytelling" timeline that highlights the quantity and level of corrupt acts, pointing at Shorthand's and Alliance Interactive's timeline roundups and at thexlist.org (a question as hook, a unit mark per item, counters, filters, evidence per mark). The concept chosen: one tally mark per act, drawn as the reader scrolls (chapter cards, then per-year steps through the first term and the gap, per-month steps through the second term), then the same marks fly into a per-month histogram with filters and a detail drawer.

**How to apply:**
- The eight color families (self-dealing, foreign/family, crypto, pay-to-play/favors, pardons, appointees, trades, enforcement/cover-up, plus gray "other") are a page-level grouping of the ledger's two label vocabularies; the mapping lives in `build.cjs` and is shown under Method. Re-labeling the ledger itself is the user's call.
- The palette (red #d03b3b, teal #1fa8a8, amber #b5820c, purple #9085e9, orange #d4701f, blue #3987e5, green #0ca30c, rose #d45f9e, in that order) passed the dataviz validator on both Deep Dives surfaces; do not reorder rose next to teal.
- Open decisions the user has not made: where it lives (a Deep Dives page needs a second seed type in `verify-sources.js` for the 166 researched items), pacing (38 steps), whether to add an "Our take", the name.
- Keep the page's coverage caveat: the second term's density partly reflects the tracker following 2026 day by day while earlier years were researched after the fact.
- **Threads (added 2026-10-26):** a Deep Dives dive followed through the count. `build.cjs` reads the dive's JSON as it is into `threads[]` (The Ballroom so far); a thread's moments never join the main count, the seeded ledger acts stay lit, and the right column shows the dive's own chapters. The user's direction the same day: retire Deep Dives as a site, make this the template (the press next, which needs a press ledger first), fold each dive in as a thread. Next dive candidates: By His Own Account (25 events, 11 acts), Paid For (29 events, 2 acts).
- **One at a time (added 2026-10-26):** `focus.html` reads the same data one act per scroll-snapped screen with the wall in ink beside it, on paper with one red (Fraunces, Source Serif 4, Plex Mono). The user said these pages need not match the other sites' theme, and wanted the reader's eye on a single act, not a list. Open: whether it becomes the front door, whether threads get the same treatment, paper versus dark.
- **Filters (added 2026-10-26):** family, tracker rating (with "not rated" explicit, since 166 acts carry none) and period, shared by both pages and carried in `?f=&r=&t=`; dropped acts stay as ghosts on the wall and as hidden screens on the one-at-a-time page. The user approved exactly this shape; status flags (borderline, alleged) were offered and not taken up. Filtering by family leans on the eight-family grouping the user has not yet signed off on.
- **Breakdown and strip (added 2026-10-26):** from ChronoFlo's monarchs timeline the user took two devices and declined the 3D road: a running breakdown by family that grows with the count (stacked bar on the dark page, ink bars on paper) and a scrubbable time strip (eras, monthly density, thread spans, red playhead) on both pages. The user's taste so far: one thing in focus, honest counts, no template looks.
- **Paper for everything, and a door (2026-10-26):** the user chose the paper look for both readings; the scroll page is now `count.html` (ink wall, family color only where labeled, a reference light palette validated on paper), `focus.html` is one at a time, and `index.html` is a door that asks what to see, which years and how to read, with a live preview, choices in the link and remembered. The pages call themselves The Count; the Deep Dives wordmark is gone from them.
- **Prologue and story mode (2026-10-26):** the user wants the story to open with how Trump set the stage (IGs fired, stock-trading ban kept off, loyalists, his lawyer at DOJ) and a story mode. A verified sweep produced `story/prologue.json` (six sections, 35 beats, 95 gated URLs). Three examples hold; the fourth was researched by a linked session on 2026-10-26 and reverses the premise: Trump backed a Congress-only stock ban (Time April 2025 "Absolutely", the 2026 State of the Union, an OMB statement for H.R. 7008) and attacked Hawley's HONEST Act, the only bill covering the president; the prologue must never say he pushed to keep Congress trading. The prologue was regenerated that evening on the user's word (43 beats, seven sections, 21 in the count; the stock-ban entry sits among the law's limits as the record shows it); rerun the same workflow shape (finders with must-reads, verifiers that check quote speakers, synthesis against the prior outline, critic, gap fill, then the assembler's URL and id gate and a refuter per quote) whenever the corpus gains setup entries. A beat whose entry has no ledger link but describes an act in the count gets a `ledgerId`. When a tracker entry a step cites is corrected (the Schedule Policy/Career entry was, 2026-10-26: the rule moved no one, about 8,000 placed by a June order), refresh that one step with a finder that reads only the entry and a verifier, splice it, re-gate, and stamp the cautions that named it with a dated note rather than rewriting them. In the corpus the "personal attorney as AG" is Todd Blanche (April 2026), not Bondi. Story mode is built as `story.html` (approved 2026-10-26, now the door's default answer): a cold open on one Appendix B figure, the 35 beats one per screen with `why` and its basis, the 14 beats outside the count on their own wall row headed "Prologue · 14 not in the count" (never "setup": they include court rulings and a Senate vote), a cautions screen rendering the sweep's gaps and cautions verbatim plus a red limits line in the panel through the prologue, then eras (days between acts with the coverage caveat), threads as interludes, totals, close. The user's rule for it: the told order, gaps and cautions always visible. A linked session (tracker-entries-stock-tr) is researching the stock-trading stance and the missing setup entries; the prologue is regenerated only on the user's word. A four-lens review the same day (56 findings) and a second two-lens round (30 more, one a blocker: a thread's lit acts are its seed entries' acts, never its moments), all fixed, set rules worth keeping: hand-written framing may never say the prologue caused the count (the outline's own framing disclaims it); a quote renders only with the speaker its entry names (`quoteBy`, verified per quote); no family is shortened to its most loaded word or to one that reverses it ("Favors and contracts", "Appointee misconduct", "Dismantling enforcement", never "Pay-to-play", "Appointees", "Enforcement", in every panel; the door says "Favors, contracts and access"; era stats quote the full ledger label); placeholder dates render as "Undated"; era pace is "days per documented act" over the era's own span with a per-era coverage line, never compared across eras; keyboard and Play must scroll a tall screen in parts; small text ink is #6b6458 on paper.
- **Template (2026-10-26, late):** the pages are masters in `src/`; `topics.cjs` holds one entry per topic (ledger path, five chapters with fixed keys, terms, eras, families with validated colors, door groups, threads with actor names, copy) and `node build.cjs` builds every topic into its folder, stamping `data-topic` and `data-prologue` on the copies. Never edit the root or `press/` copies. The press edition (`press/`) reads `trump-press-chronology.md` (197 acts, nine families in palette order B, Shut Out as its thread, cold open on Axios’s 61 suits). A new topic needs: a ledger in the same markdown shape, a topics.cjs entry, a prologue sweep into `<out>/story/` (same workflow, topic themes), then the quote refute and reader-cautions passes, then an Opus page review of the new edition in headless Chrome (the press edition exposed count strings that broke at zero or a full span, framing written for the other edition, and a double-counted act; all fixed Oct 26). Press prologue: "What Changed Around the Press", 55 steps, all acts in the count.
- **Tooling (2026-10-27):** everything that builds a prologue is in `prototypes/the-count/tools/` (seven node tools plus five workflow scripts in `tools/workflows/`), reads `topics.cjs` and takes the topic key first; each topic's sweep configuration is its `story.prologue` block in topics.cjs; `tools/README.md` is the sequence for a third edition and for refreshing a step after a correction. Run workflows with `Workflow({scriptPath, args})` where args come from `node tools/args.cjs <topic> <sweep|refute|cautions|review|beat>`. Nothing lives in a scratchpad any more.
